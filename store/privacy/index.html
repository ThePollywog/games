Privacy Policy

SHIPSHAPE · BUOY OH BUOY · KNOTS & BOLTS · HEX MARKS THE SPOT

Play · Support · Privacy

In short

Applies to the iOS apps and web versions of Shipshape, Buoy Oh Buoy, Knots & Bolts and Hex Marks the Spot, by The Pollywog. Last updated October 30, 2026.

We don't collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the games never send anything to us.

On your device

  • iOS apps: your progress, coins, achievements and settings are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Web versions: the same information is kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. The pages load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits.

Game Center

The iOS apps show leaderboards through Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and Game Center nickname are sent to Apple so they can appear on the leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy; we never receive it. You can play without signing in.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information from anyone, including children.
