# App Store listing: Shipshape

Paste into App Store Connect › Shipshape › App Information and the version page. Limits are Apple's. Keep "Free" out of the name and subtitle: guideline 2.3.7 bars pricing terms in metadata, and reviewers read "Ad Free" as one. "No ads" says the same thing safely.

## Name (30 max)

```
Shipshape: Bottle Sort
```

"Shipshape" alone is probably taken; this keeps the brand first and adds what the game is. The label under the icon stays "Shipshape".

## Description (4000 max)

```
Shipshape is a seafaring color-sorting puzzle from The Pollywog, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Ever.

Tap a bottle, tap another, and pour. Sea water only lands on its own color or in an empty bottle. Fill a bottle with a single color to cork it, and cork them back to back for combo bonuses. Get every bottle shipshape.

CAMPAIGN
Chapters from the Harbor to Davy Jones' Locker, a storm level every ten, and stars for beating a par worked out by a solver. From level 24, murky layers stay hidden until they reach the top.

DAILY CHALLENGE
One puzzle for everyone each day. Your first solve is ranked on Game Center.

BLITZ
Two minutes, endless puzzles, one weekly Game Center leaderboard.

ZEN
No timer, free hints. Pick a size from 3 to 12 colors and unwind.

FAIR BY DESIGN
• Undo is always free.
• Hints and extra bottles cost coins you earn by playing, never by paying.
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking.

ALSO
• Achievements, daily streaks, and sailor ranks: start a Pollywog, cross the line to Shellback
• Color palettes to unlock
• Color-blind symbols, reduced motion and haptics
• Plays offline
```
