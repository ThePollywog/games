## Description (4000 max)

```
Hex Marks the Spot is a hex-stacking puzzle from The Pollywog, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Ever.

Drop treasure stacks on the map. Neighbours with the same top color merge; ten of a color digs up points, and every dig that sets off another multiplies the haul. A reef rock surfaces every 20 placements, and the voyage ends when the map fills up.

ENDLESS VOYAGE
Play as long as the map holds out. Your best voyage each week is ranked on Game Center.

DAILY DIG
The same pieces for everyone each day. Your first game is ranked on Game Center.

POWER-UPS
• Cannon: blast any stack off the map (3 per game).
• Shuffle: swap out your tray (3 per game).
Both cost coins you earn by playing, never by paying.

ALSO
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking
• Achievements, daily streaks, and sailor ranks: start a Pollywog, cross the line to Shellback
• Color palettes to unlock
• Drag-and-drop or tap-to-place, color-blind symbols, reduced motion and haptics
• Plays offline
```
