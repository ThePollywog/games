## Description (4000 max)

```
Buoy Oh Buoy is a ball-sorting puzzle from The Pollywog, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Ever.

Tap a channel, then tap where its top buoy should go. One buoy moves at a time, onto its own color or into an empty channel. Fill each channel with a single color to make it fast, and every move counts against par.

CAMPAIGN
Chapters from the Marina to the Bermuda Triangle, a storm level every ten, and stars for beating a par worked out by a solver. From level 30, buoys lost in the fog keep their color hidden until they reach the top.

DAILY CHALLENGE
One puzzle for everyone each day. Your first solve is ranked on Game Center.

BLITZ
Two minutes, endless puzzles, one weekly Game Center leaderboard.

ZEN
No timer, free hints. Pick a size from 3 to 10 colors and drift.

FAIR BY DESIGN
• Undo is always free.
• Hints and extra channels cost coins you earn by playing, never by paying.
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking.

ALSO
• Achievements, daily streaks, and sailor ranks: start a Pollywog, cross the line to Shellback
• Color palettes to unlock
• Color-blind symbols, reduced motion and haptics
• Plays offline
```
