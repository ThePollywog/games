Play · Support · Privacy

Play

All four games are on the App Store, with no ads. You can also play them in your browser:

  • Pollywog Arcade (all four games)
  • Shipshape, Buoy Oh Buoy, Knots & Bolts and Hex Marks the Spot

Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug, or have an idea? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL with the game's name and your device model, and we'll get back to you.

  • Leaderboards use Game Center. If they don't load, check you're signed in under Settings › Game Center.
  • Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Coins are earned by playing. There's nothing to buy.

Privacy

The games collect no data: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Read the full privacy policy.
