Privacy Policy

Applies to Shipshape, Buoy Oh Buoy, Knots & Bolts and Hex Marks the Spot. Last updated October 29, 2026.

We don't collect your data. The games have no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts, and they never send anything to us.

  • On your device: your progress, coins, achievements and settings are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • In your browser: the web versions keep the same information in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. They load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits.
  • Game Center: if you sign in to Game Center, your scores and Game Center nickname are sent to Apple to show leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in.
  • Children: the games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: SUPPORT_EMAIL.
