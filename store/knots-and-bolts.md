## Description (4000 max)

```
Knots & Bolts is a rope-sorting puzzle from The Pollywog, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Ever.

Tap a bollard, then another: every matching rope coil on top slides across. Four coils of one color make a bollard fast. Some bollards stand taller and hold an extra coil; from level 12 there's only one spare, and the tall ones are your lifeline.

CAMPAIGN
Chapters from the Dockside to the Flying Dutchman, a storm level every ten, and stars for beating a par worked out by a solver. From level 30, tarred coils keep their color hidden until they reach the top.

DAILY CHALLENGE
One puzzle for everyone each day. Your first solve is ranked on Game Center.

BLITZ
Two minutes, endless puzzles, one weekly Game Center leaderboard.

ZEN
No timer, free hints. Pick a size from 3 to 11 colors and take it easy.

FAIR BY DESIGN
• Undo is always free.
• Hints and extra bollards cost coins you earn by playing, never by paying.
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking.

ALSO
• Achievements, daily streaks, and sailor ranks: start a Pollywog, cross the line to Shellback
• Color palettes to unlock
• Color-blind symbols, reduced motion and haptics
• Plays offline
```
